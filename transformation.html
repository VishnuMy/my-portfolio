02 / DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The registry is a component. The engine is the system.

Enterprises do not need another catalog of agents. They need a repeatable mechanism that connects business demand to architecture, evaluation, governance, economics, and operating discipline.

MCP fits inside this architecture as the interoperability layer. It standardizes access to capabilities without replacing the orchestration and transformation logic above it.
