01 / THESIS

Models are becoming commodities. Operating discipline is not.

The durable advantage in enterprise AI is shifting upward from access to models and connectors toward the systems that decide what to build, how to orchestrate it, how to evaluate it, what it costs, when humans intervene, and whether it should be in production at all.

MCP accelerates that shift by standardizing interoperability. My work focuses on what sits above the protocol layer.
